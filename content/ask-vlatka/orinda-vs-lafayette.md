**Short answer:** Both towns have BART service and hillside neighborhoods, but Lafayette has a longer commercial corridor with more daily retail and dining choices, while Orinda is primarily residential with a smaller town center split between the Village and Theatre Square. The better fit depends on the exact neighborhood and daily routine.

## Local Context

A town-level comparison is only a starting point. Walkability, road access, sun exposure, lot shape, and distance to a station can change within each town. Compare actual addresses and make the trip at the times you would normally travel.

## What to Verify

- Use the official [Lafayette BART station page](https://www.bart.gov/stations/lafy) and [Orinda BART station page](https://www.bart.gov/stations/orin) for current schedules, parking, and advisories.
- Verify school assignment directly with the district.
- Obtain current insurance information and property-specific inspections.
