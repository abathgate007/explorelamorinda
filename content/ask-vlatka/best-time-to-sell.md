**Short answer:** There is no single best month for every Lamorinda home. Seasonal buyer activity matters, but property readiness, competing inventory, pricing, school-calendar considerations, and the seller's own timing can matter more.

## Local Context

A strong launch date is one when the property, disclosures, photography, and pricing strategy are ready and the competitive set is understood. An undated rule such as “spring is always best” is too broad for a specific decision.

## What to Verify

Review current comparable sales, active listings, pending competition, and the seller's carrying costs. Ask what data supports the proposed timing and what would change the recommendation.
