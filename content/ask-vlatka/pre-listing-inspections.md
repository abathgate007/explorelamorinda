**Short answer:** Sellers often discuss general home, wood-destroying pest, roof, sewer, chimney, pool, drainage, or structural inspections, but there is no universal package. The appropriate scope depends on the property, local requirements, condition, and sale strategy.

## Local Context

Pre-listing information can reduce surprises, but an inspection is not a guarantee and may create disclosure obligations. Decide the scope with qualified local professionals and review reports carefully before marketing.

## What to Verify

Confirm jurisdiction-specific requirements, especially for sewer or transfer-related rules, with the responsible agency. Use appropriately licensed inspectors and obtain legal advice for disclosure questions.
