**Short answer:** Ask early whether the exact property is insurable, what coverage and deductibles are offered, which mitigation conditions apply, and whether additional policies are needed. Do not infer a quote from a town name or hazard-map category.

## Local Context

Insurance underwriting can consider property characteristics and proprietary risk models. Availability, price, exclusions, deductibles, and required mitigation can differ by carrier and address.

## What to Verify

Request written quotes from licensed insurance professionals for the specific property. Review the California Department of Insurance [wildfire preparation guidance](https://www.insurance.ca.gov/01-consumers/140-catastrophes/WildfireBefore.cfm). The Department also explains that CAL FIRE hazard maps are not themselves used to set insurance rates or underwriting decisions.
