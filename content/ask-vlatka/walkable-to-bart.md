**Short answer:** Parts of downtown Lafayette and central Orinda can be within walking distance of their BART stations, but a simple radius is not enough. Hills, crossings, sidewalk continuity, lighting, and the route to a specific property determine whether the walk is practical.

## Local Context

Walk the exact route in both directions. A comfortable downhill trip in the morning can be a different experience uphill after work. Also test the route after dark and consider mobility needs.

## What to Verify

Start with the official [Lafayette](https://www.bart.gov/stations/lafy) or [Orinda](https://www.bart.gov/stations/orin) station map. Then verify the public walking route and current construction conditions in person.
