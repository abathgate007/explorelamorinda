**Short answer:** Orinda has direct BART service on the Antioch to SFO/Millbrae line, but total commute time depends on the home-to-station trip, wait time, service conditions, destination, and final connection. A fixed estimate can become stale quickly.

## Local Context

Test the door-to-door journey on a representative weekday. Include parking or walking, platform time, the San Francisco station you actually need, and the last mile.

## What to Verify

Use BART's official [Orinda station page](https://www.bart.gov/stations/orin), trip planner, real-time departures, and service advisories. Recheck shortly before making a time-sensitive decision.
