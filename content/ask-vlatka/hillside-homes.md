**Short answer:** A hillside home deserves property-specific review of drainage, retaining structures, foundation behavior, access, trees, wildfire preparation, and insurance. The slope itself does not answer those questions; qualified inspections and public records do.

## Local Context

Ask how water moves across the lot, what work has been permitted, how emergency and maintenance vehicles reach the property, and which exterior areas require ongoing upkeep. Inspection scope should follow the property's age, construction, disclosures, and visible conditions.

## What to Verify

Use CAL FIRE's current [Fire Hazard Severity Zone information and map](https://osfm.fire.ca.gov/what-we-do/community-wildfire-preparedness-and-mitigation/fire-hazard-severity-zones). CAL FIRE distinguishes hazard from risk and recommends address-specific review. Consult appropriate inspectors, engineers, arborists, insurers, and public agencies.
