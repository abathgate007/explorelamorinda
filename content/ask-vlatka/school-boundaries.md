**Short answer:** A property's address can affect its K-8 district and assigned school, and a listing is not the final authority. Verify the exact address directly with the relevant district before relying on an assignment.

## Local Context

Town names, postal addresses, and school attendance areas are not interchangeable. Capacity, transfers, and district policies can also affect enrollment. Keep a dated record of the district response and reconfirm before enrollment if time has passed.

## What to Verify

Contact the [Lafayette School District](https://www.lafsd.org/), [Moraga School District](https://www.moraga.k12.ca.us/), [Orinda Union School District](https://www.orindaschools.org/), and [Acalanes Union High School District](https://www.acalanes.k12.ca.us/) as applicable. Orinda's official [residency-verification page](https://www.orindaschools.org/Enrollment/Residency-Verification/index.html) explains its documentation requirement.
