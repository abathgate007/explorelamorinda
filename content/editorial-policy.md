## Purpose

Explore Lamorinda publishes practical information about Lafayette, Moraga, and Orinda. Accuracy and transparent attribution take priority over publishing frequency.

## Sourcing

Changing claims about transit, schools, public safety, fire hazards, insurance rules, events, prices, and government requirements should link to an official or primary source and state when the information was verified. Real-estate market figures must identify their reporting period, property type, geography, and data source.

## Authorship and Review

Each substantive article should identify its author or reviewer. Vlatka Bathgate reviews only content explicitly attributed to her. Her brokerage and California DRE number appear wherever her professional role is material.

## Commentary

Opinion and humor must be clearly labeled. They must not invent identifiable people, quotations, business practices, schedules, prices, or statistics and present them as observed fact.

## AI-Assisted Drafting

AI may assist with structure or drafting, but a human must verify names, quotations, dates, numbers, schedules, local observations, and source links before publication. AI-generated anecdotes or claims may not be presented as firsthand experience.

## Commercial Relationships

Explore Lamorinda is published in association with Vlatka Bathgate, Coldwell Banker Realty, DRE #01390784. Paid placements or other material commercial relationships must be disclosed on the relevant page.

## Corrections

Send a correction through the [contact page](/contact/) with the affected URL and supporting source. Material corrections should update the page's verification date. Publication and modification dates must reflect the actual editorial history.

## Scope

The site provides general educational information. It does not replace advice from public agencies or licensed legal, tax, insurance, engineering, school, or financial professionals.
