Lamorinda combines **La**fayette, **Mor**aga, and Or**inda** — three neighboring East Bay communities east of the Caldecott Tunnel. Each has a distinct town center, residential landscape, school system, and relationship to regional transit.

Explore Lamorinda publishes practical community information for residents, visitors, buyers, and sellers.
