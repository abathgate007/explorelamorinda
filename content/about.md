## A Practical Guide to Three East Bay Communities

Explore Lamorinda covers Lafayette, Moraga, and Orinda for people who live here, visit, or are considering a move. The aim is straightforward: answer useful local questions clearly, distinguish fact from opinion, and make changing information easy to verify.

## Publisher and Affiliation

Explore Lamorinda is the publisher of this guide. It is published in association with [Vlatka Bathgate](/vlatka-bathgate/), a California real-estate professional with Coldwell Banker Realty, DRE #01390784.

Vlatka serves as the named subject-matter expert for real-estate and relocation content attributed to her. Community articles may be published by Explore Lamorinda. Every substantive article should identify its author or reviewer rather than implying anonymous firsthand reporting.

This site is not affiliated with the cities of Lafayette, Moraga, or Orinda. Editorial content is not legal, financial, insurance, school-placement, or real-estate advice.

## How We Work

- Changing facts should include a verification date and a link to an official or primary source.
- Recommendations and local observations should be identified as judgment, not presented as universal fact.
- Paid or commercial relationships must be disclosed.
- AI-assisted drafts require human fact-checking before publication.
- Invented people, quotations, observations, schedules, prices, and statistics are prohibited unless content is unmistakably labeled fiction.
- Publication and modification dates must reflect the real editorial history.

Read the full [editorial and corrections policy](/editorial-policy/).
